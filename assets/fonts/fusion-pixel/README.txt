ja_small.png, ja_label.png and ja_glyphs.lua (one folder up) are image-font atlases of the
kana and the few other characters the WoobNav's Japanese needs, rendered from Fusion Pixel
Font 8px proportional (Japanese) by TakWolf -- the face Kanto Gear draws Japanese with.
They are a Modified Version of the Font Software under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(OFL.txt here); the notices of the fonts Fusion Pixel is built from are in LICENSES/.

Source: https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font
