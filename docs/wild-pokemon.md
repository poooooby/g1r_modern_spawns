# 🌿 Wild Pokémon

How WoobleSpawns picks the Pokémon you meet, and the ways you can shape it.
[Back to the README](../README.md)

## Pokémon that belong where you find them

Each map has its own levels, terrain and habitat, and every species is picked to suit them.
That means Water types by the sea and Ghosts in Pokémon Tower and Mt. Pyre. Electric types
fill the Power Plant and New Mauville, Bugs live in the forests, Ground types in the desert and
Ice types in the frozen caves. Named places in Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and the Sevii Islands keep
their character, and late evolutions turn up in late areas.

The game itself plays the same. Each map keeps its original encounter rate, levels and odds;
only the Pokémon change.

Early routes have the fewest Pokémon that suit them, so variety grows the further you go.

## Four spawn modes

**SPAWN MODE** decides how often the wild changes.

- **COMPLETE** (the default): catch them all. Every first-stage Pokémon in your range lives
  somewhere in the wild, and no Pokémon on any map has less than a 5% chance per encounter,
  so nothing is buried behind odds of 1 in 500. Evolved Pokémon are mostly left for you to
  evolve. The game's own Pokémon keep a few of their old homes, like Pikachu in Viridian Forest
  and Zigzagoon on Route 101, without crowding every route.
- **LIMITED:** the classic feel. Each map gets one roster for your whole playthrough, picked to
  fit like the original games. Newcomers tend to share the types of the Pokémon they replace,
  so each route and cave keeps its character.
- **EVERY MAP:** a fresh roster each time you enter a map.
- **RANDOM:** a new species every encounter.

## Choosing generations

**GENERATIONS** sets which generations can appear, from GEN 1 up to GEN 1-9. GEN 1 shuffles
the original 151.

Pick **CUSTOM** for any mix: a GEN 1 … GEN 9 switch appears for each generation, so you can
play with just Gen 3 to 8, or only Gen 1, 3 and 6.

- A Pokémon from a generation you left out never appears in the wild. Your own Pokémon still
  evolve as usual, so a Chansey you catch can still become a Blissey.
- Turning every generation off, or picking only generations that no installed species mod
  adds, leaves the game's original Pokémon.

You only get the Pokémon your game has unless you add a species mod. See
[Getting started](../README.md#-getting-started).

## Seeds

Each save has its own seed, a short code of letters, so your world is your own. Type a friend's
seed into **SEED** to get the same wild Pokémon they have, or use **REROLL SEED** to draw a new
one.

## What stays the game's own

- 🎣 The Good and Super Rods catch new Pokémon too: water species only, at the game's own levels
  and odds. The Old Rod keeps its original catches everywhere.
- Rock Smash keeps its original Pokémon on FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald.
- Gold, Silver and Crystal keep their swarms, the Bug-Catching Contest, Headbutt trees and the
  roaming beasts.

## Regional forms

On FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, forms that are wild in the real games can
appear: Galarian and Hisuian forms, Flabébé's colours, Pumpkaboo's sizes and more. Mega
Evolutions and forms that need an item never do.

## Finding Pokémon

- 📖 On FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, the Pokédex AREA page shows where each
  species lives under your settings. With
  [WoobleDex](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3) you can even look up Pokémon you
  haven't seen yet.
- 📟 With Kanto Gear, the [WoobNav](woobnav.md) app lists every Pokémon on your map and its
  odds.
