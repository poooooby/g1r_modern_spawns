# 📟 WoobNav

A pocket guide to the wild Pokémon around you, inside
[Kanto Gear](https://github.com/AverageConsumer/kanto-gear).
[Back to the README](../README.md)

With Kanto Gear installed, WoobleSpawns adds a **WOOBNAV** app to the Gear's Silph Store. Get it
there, then open it from the Gear's Home screen. It appears on the Gear's own screen, in its
pixel style.

## The area list

- 🔎 **Every wild Pokémon on your map**, six to a page, with its sprite, name, the chance of
  meeting it, its levels and how you meet it (grass, cave, surfing, each rod, Rock Smash).
  In COMPLETE, a slot can hold several Pokémon, and every one of them is listed.
- 📊 **Real odds:** each percentage is that Pokémon's share of that way of meeting Pokémon here.
  It counts the game's own odds, TIME OF DAY SPAWNS and the time right now. Gold, Silver and
  Crystal show morning, day or night.
- Legendaries waiting on the map show as RARE.
- Tap the arrows or swipe to turn the page. The list follows you from map to map.

## A Pokémon's summary

Tap a Pokémon to see:

- its number, types, category, height and weight;
- its Pokédex entry;
- its base stats and evolution line (and abilities on Gen 3);
- where it turns up on this map at each time of day.

## Getting around

- The back button (top left) returns to the list, then to Kanto Gear.
- 🌙 **Light or dark:** tap the moon (top right) for night mode, and the sun to switch back.
  Your pick stays with your save.
- 🌐 **Your language:** WoobleSpawns' **LANGUAGE** option shows WoobNav in English, German,
  Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese or Brazilian Portuguese, the same languages as WoobOS and
  Kanto Gear. Pokémon names, places and Pokédex entries stay in your game's own words.
- ⚔️ In a battle, Kanto Gear takes the screen back for its battle controls. WoobNav returns on
  its own once the battle is over.
- It shows wherever Kanto Gear shows (a second screen, a combined screen or fullscreen) and
  works by touch or mouse. Your buttons stay with the game, so you can keep walking with it
  open.

## Good to know

- With MODERN SPAWNS OFF, WoobNav lists the game's own Pokémon.
- Under RANDOM it lists only the Old Rod and Rock Smash, since everything else is a new roll
  each time.
