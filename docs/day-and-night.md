# 🌙 Day and Night

With **TIME OF DAY SPAWNS** on, every map gets a day roster and a night roster.
[Back to the README](../README.md)

Hoothoot, Gastly, Murkrow and the other night owls come out after dark. Pidgey, Ledyba and
Sunkern keep to the daytime.

## PREFER or STRICT

- **PREFER:** Pokémon are more common at their own time and rarer at the other, but you can
  still meet any of them at any hour. In COMPLETE, even an off-hours Pokémon keeps at least a
  5% chance.
- **STRICT:** like Gold, Silver and Crystal. Night-only Pokémon appear only at night, and
  day-only ones only by day. COMPLETE still lets you catch every first-stage Pokémon at some
  hour.

## Whose clock

Night runs from 8 PM to 4 AM.

- **Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald** follow [WoobleDex](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3)'s
  Clock Source setting: the game's own clock or your device's.
- **FireRed, LeafGreen, Red, Blue and Yellow** use your device's clock.
- **Gold, Silver and Crystal** use their own clock, where night starts at 6 PM.

## Good to know

- Which Pokémon keep to the day or the night comes from the games that have a clock: Gold,
  Silver, Crystal, Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver. Newer Pokémon lean the
  way their Pokédex entries describe, and STRICT only rules out what those games did.
- On FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, the Pokédex AREA page lists every place a
  species lives, by day or at night.
