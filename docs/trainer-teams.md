# 🥊 Trainer Teams

With **TRAINER TEAMS** on, the trainers you battle field Pokémon from the generations you allow.
[Back to the README](../README.md)

## Teams that still feel right

Trainers, Gym Leaders, the Elite Four and your rival get Pokémon picked to fit their original
teams. Every Gym Leader and Elite Four member keeps their type, so Brock still throws Rock
types and Phoebe Ghosts, and they favour the stronger Pokémon of that type for their level.
🌋 Team Magma and 🌊 Team Aqua keep their element too: Maxie, his admins and his grunts send out
Fire and Ground types and never a Water one, while Archie's side fields Water types and never a
Fire one. Levels and team sizes stay the game's own, and legendaries never join a trainer's team.

TRAINER TEAMS works whether MODERN SPAWNS is on or off. It follows your save's seed, so the same
seed always gives the same teams.

## A rival who grows with you

- Each of your rival's Pokémon becomes an evolution line that stays the same in every fight and
  evolves as its level rises. Rematches and repeat trainers keep their lines too.
- ⚖️ **A fair first battle:** your rival leads with a modern starter that YOUR starter beats
  (pick a Grass starter and your rival leads with a Water one). It evolves exactly when the
  original rival's starter would.

## The end game too

- Crystal's Battle Tower.
- Emerald's Battle Frontier: all seven facilities, the Battle Tents, and the wild Pokémon of the
  Battle Pyramid and Battle Pike. The Battle Factory's rentals are modern too.
- Emerald's Trainer Hill, Ruby and Sapphire's Battle Tower, and FireRed and LeafGreen's Trainer
  Tower.

Each Pokémon there keeps its set's item, nature and training, and gets moves of its own species.

- Switching TRAINER TEAMS in the middle of a challenge changes the opponents still to come.
- With only a few generations allowed there are fewer Pokémon to swap in, so the facilities
  change less.

## 🎒 Held items (ON + ITEMS)

Items grow with your journey, so the first battles stay fair:

- **Your rival's first battle** and the trainers before the third Gym hold nothing new.
- **Gym Leaders, your rival and the villain bosses:** their strongest Pokémon always holds
  something, and more of their team joins in as you go. That means a Berry or a type booster at
  the first Gyms, Leftovers mid-game, and standouts like Life Orb or a Choice item from about
  Lv 35.
- **Other trainers** later on carry one item, then sometimes two: a Berry or a type booster.
- **The Elite Four, the Champion and your rival's last battle:** every Pokémon holds one.
- Items the original trainers held stay, and no team holds two of the same item.

Newer items such as Life Orb, Choice Specs, Focus Sash, Black Sludge, Eviolite and Assault Vest
turn up once GENERATIONS includes their generation. They need
[WoobleDex](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3) installed to work. Your game's own
items are always in play.

Each item goes to a Pokémon it suits: Black Sludge to Poison types, Eviolite to one that can
still evolve, Choice Band to an all-physical attacker and Choice Specs to an all-special one.
With WoobleDex's Move Split on, physical and special go by each move, just like the battles do
(Shadow Ball special, Crunch physical).

Red, Blue and Yellow's trainers can't hold items, so ON + ITEMS plays like ON there.

## Good to know

- With CUSTOM generations, trainers skip a stage from a generation you left out. With only
  Gen 2 and 4, for example, a trainer's Happiny grows straight into Blissey.
- Teams from other players (record mixing, secret bases), e-Reader trainers and link battles
  stay as they are.
