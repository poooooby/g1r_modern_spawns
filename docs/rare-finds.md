# 👑 Rare Finds

Legendaries in the wild and shiny chains.
[Back to the README](../README.md)

## Legendaries in the wild

With **LEGENDARIES** on, legendary and mythical Pokémon can turn up after you've entered the
Hall of Fame. Each has a few fitting home maps.

- They're very rare there: 1 in 1024 encounters for a legendary, 1 in 2048 for a mythical.
- They stop appearing once you own one.
- On FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, their Pokédex AREA page shows where to look
  before you've seen them.

Outside of this, legendaries and mythicals never appear in the wild.

## ✨ Shiny chains

Win against or catch the same species again and again to build a chain:

- **It turns up more often**, at every encounter from a chain of 9.
- **It's likelier to be shiny**, about 1 in 82 at a chain of 100.
- **It gains perfect stats:** 1, 2 or 3 of them from a chain of 10, 25 or 40.

Running from it or blacking out ends the chain. Turning **SHINY CHAINS** off pauses it, and your
chain is kept.

On FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald,
[Wilds of Hoenn](https://github.com/poooooby/wilds-of-hoenn) adds the shinies and stats.
